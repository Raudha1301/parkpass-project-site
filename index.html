About the project

Problem Statement: Bus ticketing in Uganda is largely manual — passengers queue physically to purchase tickets and operators have little visibility into bookings and seat availability.

Our Solution: ParkPass is a mobile bus ticketing platform built using Flutter and Supabase. Passengers can search routes, book seats, and manage tickets from their phones.

How ParkPass Works

Today, boarding an intercity bus in Kampala means arriving early, physically queueing at the terminal, and negotiating a fare on the spot with no guarantee a seat is actually available by the time you reach the counter. Cargo owners face the same uncertainty: they show up with goods and only then find out whether there's space and what it will cost. ParkPass moves all of that onto the passenger's phone, before they ever leave home.

A conductor first registers their company and is approved by a company admin. Once approved, they publish routes and schedule trips — each trip carries its own bus class, plate number, driver details, and seat count. From there, passengers can search, compare, and book without ever speaking to anyone in person.

Booking a Ticket, Step by Step

Phasellus viverra nulla is not how a ParkPass booking works — here's the real flow, from the ParkPass GitHub repository through to boarding:

  1. Search — the passenger picks a company (or searches across all of them) and looks up trips by destination.

  2. Select a seat — a live, colour-coded seat map shows what's free, held, or already booked, updating in real time as other passengers book.

  3. Pay — via Mobile Money or card, with the fare shown up front, no negotiation required.

  4. Board — the passenger's QR ticket, generated instantly and cached offline, gets scanned by the conductor at boarding.

What a Trip Actually Includes

  • Origin, destination, and departure time
  • Bus class: Ordinary, Executive, or VIP
    • Each class can carry its own fare, set by the conductor when scheduling the trip
    • Bus plate number, colour, and driver contact are attached to every trip
  • Remaining cargo capacity, tracked separately from passenger seats

Booking Flow

QR ticket placeholder Once payment succeeds, ParkPass generates a QR ticket immediately and caches it on the device using SQLite. That means the ticket still opens and displays at the terminal even with zero signal — a real constraint at many upcountry bus parks in Uganda where network coverage can be unreliable right when it matters most. When the conductor scans it, ParkPass checks that the ticket belongs to that exact trip, is actually paid for, and hasn't already been used, before marking the passenger checked in. Any mismatch shows a clear red warning instead of silently letting someone board twice on one ticket.

Built for Real Bus Parks, Not Just Demos

ParkPass is designed around how Kampala intercity travel actually works today — cash fares negotiate d on the spot, unreliable schedules, and cargo that shows up with no warning. Every feature maps back to one of those real problems.

Core Features

Search & Book by Route
Passengers search by origin/destination or browse a specific company, see upcoming trips with bus class, fare, and remaining seats, and book directly from the results.

Live Seat Selection
A colour-coded seat grid updates in real time as other passengers book, with automatic release of abandoned holds so no seat gets stuck unavailable forever.

QR Tickets, Even Offline
Every paid booking generates a scannable QR ticket, cached on-device so it still opens with zero signal at the terminal.

Cargo Booking & Verification
Cargo owners book space on a trip by weight, see the price computed instantly, and the conductor verifies capacity before it's ever brought to the terminal.

Conductor Tools
Create routes and trips, set bus plate/colour/class and driver details, manage delays or cancellations, and scan tickets to check passengers in at boarding.

Company Admin Dashboard
Register a bus company, approve conductor applications, and track routes, trips, and approved conductors at a glance.
